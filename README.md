# Hello! This is EasyNits! ツ

EasyNits is a simple brightness management tool designed for comfort, automation, and personalization. The app includes features like \"Dynamic Nits Control\" with fast ambient light detection for responsive, smooth transitions, \"Personalized Offsets\" to manually fine-tune the brightness baseline to suit your comfort level, and \"Scheduled Time Frames\" to automatically apply specific brightness offsets for different times of the day (e.g., a dimmer display at night) - open, for free, and with no ads.

## Features

### 💡 Core Brightness Management

- Dynamic Nits Control: Uses your device's ambient light sensor to provide responsive and smooth brightness transitions.
- Manual Brightness Offset: A dedicated slider and step-buttons allow you to manually fine-tune the brightness baseline (±100%) to suit your immediate comfort level.
- Intelligent Mapping: Uses a logarithmic curve and gamma correction (1.8 gamma) to ensure mid-range responsiveness that matches how human eyes perceive light.
- Dark Environment Optimization: Guaranteed minimum baseline brightness even in pitch-black conditions, ensuring the offset controls remain effective at night.

### 🕒 Automation & Reliability
- Scheduled Time Frames: Automatically apply specific brightness offsets at different times of the day (e.g., a dimmer display starting at 10 PM).
- Weekday Customization: Choose specific days of the week for each scheduled offset.
- Direct Boot Support: The app starts regulating brightness as soon as the system reaches the lock screen after a reboot, even before you enter your PIN for the first time.
- Smart Background Service: A battery-friendly foreground service ensures the app stays active and responsive without being killed by the system.

### 🎨 Personalization & Theming
- Material 3 Design: A modern, clean UI built with Jetpack Compose that follows the latest Android design standards.
- Material You (Dynamic Color): Automatically adapts the app's color palette to match your device's wallpaper (Android 12+).
- Custom Color Palettes: Manually choose your own Primary, Text, and Background colors if you prefer a static look.
- Adaptive App Icons: Choose from different app icon styles to match your homescreen aesthetic.
- Dark & Light Modes: Full support for Dark, Light, and System-default themes.

### 📱 User Experience & Accessibility
- Home Screen Widget: A Material 3 Glance widget to adjust your brightness offset directly from the home screen.
- Adaptive Layouts: Optimized interface for both Portrait and Landscape orientations, with centered controls for wider screens.
- Multi-Language Support: Auto translated in some languages, including French, German, Japanese, and more.
- Permission Guidance: Integrated alerts and quick-actions to help you grant the necessary "Modify System Settings" and "Notification" permissions.
